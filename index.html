FIRST · THE SPINE

Six ideas the whole book hangs on

If you remember nothing else, remember these. Every chapter is an elaboration of one of them.

知

Know self & other

Self-knowledge plus reading the other side removes most of the risk. Know neither and you lose every time.

全

Win without fighting

The supreme skill is breaking resistance without a battle. The cheapest victory is the one nobody had to bleed for.

詐

All is deception

Manage perception. Appear weak when strong, far when near. What people expect, you can exploit.

先

Win first, then fight

The good strategist arranges things so victory is settled before the first move. Then the fight just confirms it.

水

Be like water

Avoid strength, strike weakness. Keep no fixed shape — adapt your tactics to whatever is in front of you.

速

Prize speed

No nation ever profited from a drawn-out war. Decide, move, and don't let any contest outlast its value.
